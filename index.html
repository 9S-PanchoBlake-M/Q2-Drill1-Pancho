Beginner's Guide to Muay Thai

Basic Rules / Fouls:

-No Headbutts
-No Biting or eye-gouging
-No Strikes to the groin or hitting a downed opponent
-No Strikes to the spine or direct kicks to the knee joints
-No Wrestling, hip-throws, or Judo-style sweeps using the hip or hooking behind the leg improperly
-No Holding the ropes for leverage or defense

Equipment:

-Gloves
-Handwraps
-Protective gear

Allowed Strikes / Weapons:

-Fists
-Elbows
-Knees
-Shins & Feet
-Clinching
